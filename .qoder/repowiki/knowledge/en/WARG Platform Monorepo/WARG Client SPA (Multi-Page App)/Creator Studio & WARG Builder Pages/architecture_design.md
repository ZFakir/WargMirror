Three sibling static HTML entry points share a common app-shell layout (left sidebar navigation, topbar with search/notifications/profile, optional right friends panel) and load shared CSS (`styles/tokens.css`, `styles/home.css`, plus `studio.css` or `game.css` + `edit_warg.css`) and shared scripts (`scripts/config.js`, `scripts/api.js`, `scripts/home.js`).

- `studio.html` is the creator dashboard: it lists published/unpublished Wargs via `GameCard.renderRow()` from `scripts/components/GameCard.js` and boots `scripts/studio.js` as an ES module.
- `create_warg.html` and `edit_warg.html` are near-duplicate builder canvases. Both render a `#game-map` container (nodes/edges injected by JS), a header area with editable title/description and a banner image, a toolbar (`Add Waypoint`, `Save Draft` / `Save Changes`, `Publish WARG`), and a right-side editor panel that switches between `waypoint-editor` and `edge-editor` views. They differ mainly in button labels, initial banner content, and edit page's skeleton loader (`builder-header-skeleton` vs direct fields).
- Both builder pages include modals for game-type selection (`game-selector-modal-overlay`), per-game configuration (QnA modal, Barcode modal with `html5-qrcode` scanner loaded from unpkg), plus generic alert/confirm modals.
- The runtime dependency direction is one-way: these pages depend on shared modules under `scripts/` and `scripts/components/`; they do not import each other.
