Each child module owns one or more top-level `.html` files plus a sibling `scripts/<page>.js`, `styles/<page>.css`, and `tests/<page>.spec.js`. Cross-cutting concerns are centralized in shared modules under `scripts/`: `api.js` (HTTP client), `db.js` (IndexedDB wrapper used both by pages and the service worker), `config.js` (runtime config), and reusable UI components under `scripts/components/` (CameraCapture, ConfirmModal, FlagModal, GameCard, MapModal, PlayModal, PublishModal, RemoveModal, minigame-handlers). A single `sw.js` service worker at the app root registers a versioned cache (`warg-cache-v4`) and applies three routing strategies — network-first for auth/session endpoints, stale-while-revalidate for catalogue/minigames/library data, and cache-first for static assets and map tiles — plus a Background Sync pipeline that replays pending camera-capture attempts stored via `db.js`'s `offlineDB` and broadcasts results through a `BroadcastChannel('warg_sync_channel')`. All pages are served statically (via `serve` / `http-server`) and exercised end-to-end by Playwright specs under `tests/`.
