Design Philosophy
The visual language should support exploration rather than compete with it. Every design decision should 
help players navigate, understand, and enjoy the experience without unnecessary complexity.
Exploration First Clarity Over Decoration Consistency Builds Trust Gameplay Comes First Design for Growth
ENCOURAGE CURIOSITY AND DISCOVERY THROUGH CLEAR 
NAVIGATION AND MEANINGFUL INTERACTIONS.
PRIORITISE USABILITY AND READABILITY BEFORE VISUAL 
COMPLEXITY.
REUSABLE PATTERNS SHOULD BE PREFERRED OVER 
UNIQUE SOLUTIONS.
THE INTERFACE SHOULD SUPPORT THE GAME—NOT 
DISTRACT FROM IT.
